THE OPERATIONAL RECOVERY MANUAL

Build a life in which relapse becomes harder to initiate, easier to interrupt, and less capable of becoming a full collapse.

Dark Recovery is a practical manual built around systems, constraints, environmental design, and predictable human failure. It assumes motivation will change. It helps you build safeguards for the moments when clear thinking is not available.

This manual assumes motivation will fluctuate

Do not build a system that depends on permanently feeling the way you feel today. A workable recovery system has to keep functioning when you are tired, private, angry, bored, nostalgic, overconfident, or close to access.

The point is not to distrust yourself as a person. The point is to stop placing the whole structure on a version of you that may not be present during the next hard hour.

Motivation is useful when it appears. It can open a window, make a difficult first action possible, or help you tell the truth sooner. But motivation is weather, not infrastructure. A recovery system should not collapse just because the weather changes.

This manual therefore treats safeguards as ordinary design. A rail on a stairway is not an insult to your balance. A checklist in an aircraft is not an insult to a pilot. A rule made while clearheaded is not an insult to your autonomy. It is a way of protecting the decision from conditions that routinely distort decisions.

The five-stage model

Stabilize

Reduce immediate danger and shorten the time horizon.

Restrict

Make destructive action slower, less private, and easier to interrupt.

Replace

Identify the function substance use served and build lower-risk substitutes.

Reinforce

Create floors, routines, defaults, and reviews that survive fading motivation.

Repair

Respond to lapses without minimizing them or expanding them.

What this manual does

  • Reduces dangerous improvisation.
  • Adds friction between impulse and action.
  • Helps you recognize permission-seeking thoughts.
  • Defines a Minimum Viable Day.
  • Builds replacements for the function substances served.
  • Prepares for success-related overconfidence.
  • Responds to lapses without expanding them.

What this manual does not ask

You do not need to publicly adopt a permanent identity, perform optimism, believe in a specific spiritual model, treat shame as proof of responsibility, or solve every part of life at once. Those approaches help some people. This manual is for the person who needs plain operating guidance without making recovery into a public role.

You also do not need to decide that one method is the only legitimate method. Meetings, therapy, medication, medical care, peer support, spiritual practice, mutual aid, and personal planning can all help different people in different ways. Dark Recovery is not a replacement for care that is working. It is a manual for building practical safeguards around predictable failure states.

Read it sequentially when you can. Use the 2 A.M. Protocol when you cannot. Build the Operating Plan when you have enough clarity to write down decisions you do not want to improvise later.

How to read it

If you are in the first unstable stretch, begin with The First 72 Hours and the Minimum Viable Day. If a craving is active, go directly to Cravings Are Weather or the 2 A.M. Protocol. If you keep finding private exceptions, read Do Not Trust Future You and The Permission Cascade. If the problem seems to appear only in certain rooms, routes, apps, cash patterns, or hours, read Environment Beats Intention. If stopping leaves a blank space, read What Was the Substance Doing?

After the immediate risk lowers, move to Floors Before Systems and When Things Start Going Well. These chapters are about maintenance, not inspiration. They protect the structure from overexpansion and overconfidence. If a lapse has already happened, read A Lapse Is Not a Permission Slip before the event becomes a larger collapse.

You can read the manual in one evening, but you do not have to use it all at once. The sequence exists so the ideas stack cleanly. Stabilize first. Restrict access next. Replace the function. Reinforce the floor. Repair without delay. When in doubt, return to the stage that matches the actual problem in front of you.

Build a plan that belongs to you

The Operating Plan builder turns the manual into a private document you can use during high-risk moments. It requires no account, sends nothing to a server, saves only in the current browser, and can be printed, exported, imported, or erased.

The plan is not meant to be beautiful. It is meant to be available. Use short phrases. Use roles or initials instead of full names if that feels safer. Write the message you can send when you do not want to explain everything. Write the first safeguard, not the perfect safeguard.

A good plan should answer practical questions before they become urgent: what decision is closed, what warning signs matter, which windows are high risk, what minimum day protects the floor, what craving sequence comes first, what access needs friction, what the substance was doing, who or what interrupts secrecy, what happens after a lapse, and what sign means the plan needs review.

Do not wait until the plan feels complete before it becomes useful. A partial plan with a central decision, a craving protocol, one contact option, one environmental safeguard, and one lapse sequence is already better than private improvisation. You can revise it as the manual gives you better language.

Safety boundary

Dark Recovery provides educational material and planning tools. It cannot diagnose withdrawal, assess overdose, replace professional treatment, or provide emergency response.

Abruptly stopping heavy or prolonged alcohol use can cause medically dangerous withdrawal. Abruptly stopping or rapidly reducing benzodiazepines can cause serious withdrawal, including potentially life-threatening reactions. Do not use this website as a detox plan.

Safety information is grounded in official sources from NIAAA, FDA, CDC, NIDA, SAMHSA, and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. See Sources and safety information.
