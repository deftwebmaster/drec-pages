THE OPERATIONAL RECOVERY MANUAL

Build a life in which relapse becomes harder to initiate, easier to interrupt, and less capable of becoming a full collapse.

Dark Recovery is a practical manual built around systems, constraints, environmental design, and predictable human failure. It assumes motivation will change. It helps you build safeguards for the moments when clear thinking is not available.

This manual assumes motivation will fluctuate

Do not build a system that depends on permanently feeling the way you feel today. A workable recovery system has to keep functioning when you are tired, private, angry, bored, nostalgic, overconfident, or close to access.

Motivation is useful when it appears, but it is weather, not infrastructure. The point is not to distrust yourself — it is to stop placing the whole structure on a version of you that may not be present during the next hard hour.

A rail on a stairway is not an insult to your balance. A checklist in an aircraft is not an insult to a pilot. A rule made while clearheaded is not an insult to your autonomy — it protects the decision from conditions that routinely distort decisions.

The five-stage model

Stabilize

Reduce immediate danger and shorten the time horizon.

Restrict

Make destructive action slower, less private, and easier to interrupt.

Replace

Identify the function substance use served and build lower-risk substitutes.

Reinforce

Create floors, routines, defaults, and reviews that survive fading motivation.

Repair

Respond to lapses without minimizing them or expanding them.

What this manual does

  • Reduces dangerous improvisation.
  • Adds friction between impulse and action.
  • Helps you recognize permission-seeking thoughts.
  • Defines a Minimum Viable Day.
  • Builds replacements for the function substances served.
  • Prepares for success-related overconfidence.
  • Responds to lapses without expanding them.

What it does not ask

  • Publicly adopt a permanent identity.
  • Perform optimism.
  • Believe in a specific spiritual model.
  • Treat shame as proof of responsibility.
  • Solve every part of life at once.

Meetings, therapy, medication, peer support, and personal planning can all help. Dark Recovery is not a replacement for care that is working — it is a manual for the predictable failure states in between.

How to read it

Read it sequentially when you can. Use the 2 A.M. Protocol when you cannot.

In the first unstable stretch

Start with The First 72 Hours and The Minimum Viable Day.

A craving is active right now

Go to Cravings Are Weather or the 2 A.M. Protocol.

You keep finding private exceptions

Read Do Not Trust Future You and The Permission Cascade.

The problem only appears in certain rooms, routes, apps, or hours

Read Environment Beats Intention.

Stopping leaves a blank space

Read What Was the Substance Doing?

Immediate risk has lowered

Move to Floors Before Systems and When Things Start Going Well.

A lapse already happened

Read A Lapse Is Not a Permission Slip before it becomes a larger collapse.

You can read the manual in one evening, but you do not have to use it all at once. Stabilize first. Restrict access next. Replace the function. Reinforce the floor. Repair without delay.

Build a plan that belongs to you

The Operating Plan builder turns the manual into a private document for high-risk moments. No account, nothing sent to a server — saved only in this browser, and printable, exportable, or erasable at any time.

It is not meant to be beautiful. It is meant to be available. Use short phrases, roles or initials instead of names, and the first safeguard rather than the perfect one. A good plan answers the practical questions before they become urgent:

  • What decision is closed
  • What warning signs matter
  • Which windows are high risk
  • What the minimum day protects
  • What craving sequence comes first
  • What access needs friction
  • What the substance was doing
  • Who interrupts secrecy
  • What happens after a lapse
  • What sign means the plan needs review

Do not wait for it to feel complete. A central decision, a craving protocol, one contact option, one environmental safeguard, and one lapse sequence is already better than private improvisation.

Safety boundary

Dark Recovery provides educational material and planning tools. It cannot diagnose withdrawal, assess overdose, replace professional treatment, or provide emergency response.

Abruptly stopping heavy or prolonged alcohol use can cause medically dangerous withdrawal. Abruptly stopping or rapidly reducing benzodiazepines can cause serious withdrawal, including potentially life-threatening reactions. Do not use this website as a detox plan.

Safety information is grounded in official sources from NIAAA, FDA, CDC, NIDA, SAMHSA, and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. See Sources and safety information.
